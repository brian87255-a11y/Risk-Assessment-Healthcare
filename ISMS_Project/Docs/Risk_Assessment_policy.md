# Risk Assessment Policy 

**Organization:** Tri-State Health Initiative (TSHI) Document ID: TSHI-POL-RA-001 Version: 1.0 Status: Active Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Review Date: January 1, 2027 Owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) 
--- 

**Purpose** 
---

The purpose of the this policy is to establish the requirements and responsibilities for conducting information security risk assessment ay Tri-State Health Initiative (TSHI). Risk assessment are a critical component of TSHI's information security program and are required to: 

> Identify threats to confidentiality, Integrity, and availability of electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) and other sensitive organizational data
> Evaluated the likeihood and potential impact of identified risk
> Support informed decision-making regarding security investments and control implementation
> Demonstrate compliance with the HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR §164.308(a)(1)) and other applicable regulations

This policy adopts NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 as the standard methodology for conduction all risk assessments at TSHI. 

**Scope**
--- 
This policy applies to: 

| In Scope | Description |
| :--- | :---: |
| All TSHI information systems | Including clinical, administrative, and operational systems |
| All locations | Main hospital campus and all three outpatient clinic locations |
| All data types | Electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI), Personally Identifiable Information (PII), and financial data |
| All staff | Employees, contractors, vendors, and third parties with access to TSHI systems |
| Cloud and on-premise infrastructure | All systems regardless of hosting environment |

| Out of Scope | Description |
| :--- | :---: |
| Physical security assessments | Covered under a separate Physical Security Policy |
| Third-party vendor audits | Covered under the Vendor Risk Management Program | 

**Policy Statements**
--- 

**Risk Assessment Requirement** 
TSHI must conduct a formal information security risk assessment at least **annually.** The assessment must follow the NIST SP 800-30 Rev. 1 methodology and mist be documented in a format that can be reviewed by auditors and regulators. 

**Triggered Assessments** 
In addition to annual assessment, a risk assessment must be initiated when any of the following events occurs: 

| Out of Scope | Description |
| :--- | :---: |
| New system implementation | Any new clinical, administrative, or infrastructure system introduced to the environment  |
| Significant system change | Major upgrades, migrations, or reconfigurations of existing systems|
| Merger or acquisition | Addition of new facilities, staff, or infrastructure through organizational growth |
| Security incident  | Any confirmed breach or significant security event affecting TSHI systems or data |
| Regulatory change | Introduction of new laws or regulations affecting TSHI's security obligations |
| New third-party relationship | Onboarding of vendors or partners with access to TSHI systems or ePHI|

**Documentation Requirements** 
All risk assessments **must** produce the following documentation: 

| Document | Description |
| :--- | :---: |
| Asset Inventory |  A complete list of in-scope systems and data assets  |
| Threat Identification |  Documented threat sources and threat event relevant to TSHI  |
| Vulnerability Identification |  Known weakness identified in systems and processes  |
| Risk Register |  Consolidated register of all identified risks with likelihood, impact, and risk rating |
| Treatment Recommendations |  Recommended controls or actions to address identified risks |

**Risk Acceptance** 

Risks that are not remediated must be formally accepted. Risk acceptance **must:** 

- Be documented in the Risk Register with a clear justification
- Be reviewed and approved in writing by the CISO or the executive leadership
- Be reviewed at the next annual assessment to determine if acceptance remains appropriate
